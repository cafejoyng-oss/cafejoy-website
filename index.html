The Joy Behind the Food

Cafe Joy was born from a simple need. When I first moved to Karshi with my family, I found myself constantly searching for sweet treats, tasty bites, and great food — and coming up empty every time.

So I took matters into my own hands. I trained myself, picked up skills in baking and cooking, and started making the things I craved — for myself, for family, for friends. The feedback was overwhelming. "You should sell this." So I did.

Today, Cafe Joy is the business I once looked for — delivering delicious, neatly packaged food with genuine care and great service to the Karu-Karshi axis.
